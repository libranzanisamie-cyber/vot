Think before you borrow.

Fast loans can come with fake approvals, hidden fees, and threats to your contacts. Learn the red flags in minutes, before you tap “Apply.”

Who we are

LoanWise PH is a student awareness project that teaches people how to tell legitimate lenders from scams and abusive lending practices.

Stay Safe

Avoid suspicious loan apps. Check if a lender is registered with the SEC first.

Borrow Wisely

Read the terms, interest, fees, and penalties. Borrow only what you can repay.

Protect Data

Never give an app access to your contacts, photos, or messages without a good reason.

Stop and check: 5 quick red flags

  • “No documents needed!” but fees show up later.
  • Pay first, loan later. Legit lenders deduct fees from the loan.
  • Asks for contacts, photos, and messages. They may use them to shame you.
  • Threats and public shaming if you miss a payment.
  • No verifiable company information or SEC registration.
